Frequently asked questions

Quick answers — or ask CardGuard for more detail.

What's the difference between freezing and blocking?

Freezing pauses your card and can be undone any time. Blocking cancels it permanently and a new card with a new number is issued.

How long does a replacement card take?

Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days, branch pickup 2–3 days, and express courier 1–2 days (Colombo & suburbs).

Am I liable for fraudulent transactions?

If you report promptly and haven't shared your PIN or OTP, you're generally not liable for transactions made after reporting.

How long do I have to dispute a charge?

Within 60 days of the transaction date. Most disputes are resolved within 30–45 days.
