## Devpost description (≤150 words)
> **CardGuard** is an AI support agent for retail banking that helps customers who have lost a card, had it stolen or spotted a suspicious transaction. Panicked customers often wait on hold while fraud continues. CardGuard secures the card in under a minute. A pre-chat form captures the issue and urgency. Guided workflows freeze or block the card, order a replacement and open disputes, each returning a reference number. All answers are grounded in the bank's own policy content, and the agent openly admits when it doesn't know something. Its distinctive feature is risk-based escalation: high-value, multi-transaction or OTP-compromise cases go straight to a fraud specialist with a structured summary, so customers never repeat themselves. Built with eGain using a fictional bank and demo data. It never asks for a PIN, CVV or OTP.
