# Card Replacement: Lanka Trust Bank (fictional)

> Demo knowledge-base article for CardGuard. All values are sample data.

## How long does a replacement card take?
| Delivery option | Timeline | Fee (sample) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard delivery to registered address | 5–7 business days | Free (first replacement per year) |
| Branch pickup | 2–3 business days | Free |
| Express courier (Colombo & suburbs) | 1–2 business days | LKR 1,000 |

A second or later replacement within 12 months costs **LKR 500** (debit) or **LKR 1,000** (credit).

## Will my new card have a new number?
Yes. After a permanent block, the replacement card has a **new card number, expiry date and CVV**. Your account number does not change.

## Can I deliver the card to a different address?
For security, replacement cards are delivered only to your **registered address** or a **branch** of your choice. To change your registered address, visit a branch with your NIC or passport.

## How do I activate my new card?
In LTB Mobile, go to Cards → Activate Card, or make a chip-and-PIN transaction at any ATM.

## How do I check my replacement status?
Ask CardGuard "Where is my new card?" and give your reference number, or check LTB Mobile → Cards → Card Requests.

## Can I use my card before the replacement arrives?
You can add your new card to a digital wallet as soon as it is issued in LTB Mobile, usually within 24 hours, even before the physical card arrives.
