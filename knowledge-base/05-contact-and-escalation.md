# Contact a Specialist: Lanka Trust Bank (fictional)

> Demo knowledge-base article for CardGuard. All contact details below are **demonstration values**, not real.

## How can I reach a person?
| Channel | Best for | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| **Live chat with a Fraud Specialist** | Confirmed fraud, unauthorized transactions, account takeover | 24/7 |
| **24-hour Card Hotline:** +94 11 000 0000 (demo) | Urgent card blocking, lost/stolen cards | 24/7 |
| **Request a callback** | Disputes, follow-ups, non-urgent questions | Callback within 2 hours, 8am–8pm |
| **Email:** cardcare@lankatrustbank.example (demo) | Sending dispute evidence and documents | Reply within 1 business day |
| **Visit a branch** | Address changes, card collection, ID verification | Mon–Fri 9am–3pm |

## When will CardGuard connect me to a person automatically?
- You report transactions you did not make
- The amount involved is above LKR 100,000
- There are signs someone else has access to your account
- You ask to speak to a person
- CardGuard can't resolve your question after two attempts

## What will the specialist already know?
CardGuard passes on a summary: the issue type, the last 4 digits of the card, the steps already completed (such as card blocked), your reference number and the chat transcript. You won't need to repeat yourself.
