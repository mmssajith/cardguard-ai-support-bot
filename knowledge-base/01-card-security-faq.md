# Card Security FAQ: Lanka Trust Bank (fictional)

> Demo knowledge-base article for CardGuard. Lanka Trust Bank is a fictional bank. All numbers, timelines and fees are sample values.

## Will Lanka Trust Bank ever ask for my PIN, CVV, OTP or password?
**No. Never.** Lanka Trust Bank will never ask for your PIN, the 3-digit CVV on the back of your card, a one-time password (OTP) or your online banking password, whether by phone, SMS, email, chat or social media. If anyone asks for these, it is a scam. End the conversation and report it to us.

## What should I do if I think my card details have been compromised?
1. Temporarily freeze your card right away in the LTB Mobile app (Cards → Freeze), or ask CardGuard to block it.
2. Review your recent transactions.
3. Report any transaction you don't recognise as a suspicious transaction.
4. If you confirm fraud, we will permanently block the card and send you a replacement.

## What is the difference between freezing and blocking a card?
- **Freeze (temporary):** The card is paused. No new purchases or withdrawals are approved. You can unfreeze it any time. This is useful if you think you've misplaced your card.
- **Block (permanent):** The card is cancelled for good and can't be reactivated. A new card with a new number is issued. Use this if your card is lost for certain, stolen or used fraudulently.

## Are recurring payments affected when I block my card?
Yes. Recurring payments and subscriptions linked to the old card number will fail after a permanent block. Update them with your new card details once it arrives.

## What is phishing and how do I spot it?
Phishing is when criminals pretend to be the bank to steal your details. Warning signs:
- Urgent messages like "your account will be closed today"
- Links to websites that look like ours but have a slightly different address
- Requests for your OTP, PIN or password
- Unexpected calls asking you to "verify" a transaction by reading out a code

The official Lanka Trust Bank website is **www.lankatrustbank.example** (demo domain).

## Am I liable for fraudulent transactions?
If you report a lost or stolen card promptly and have not shared your PIN or OTP, you are generally **not liable** for unauthorized transactions made after you report it. Transactions made before you report it are reviewed case by case by our Fraud team.

## How do I enable transaction alerts?
In LTB Mobile, go to Settings → Notifications → Card Alerts and turn on SMS and push alerts for every transaction. We strongly recommend this.

## Can I use my card abroad?
Yes. Turn on international usage in LTB Mobile (Cards → Usage Controls) before you travel. International usage is off by default for your safety.
