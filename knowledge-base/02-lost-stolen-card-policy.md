# Lost or Stolen Card Policy: Lanka Trust Bank (fictional)

> Demo knowledge-base article for CardGuard. All values are sample data.

## How do I report a lost or stolen card?
You can report it 24/7 through:
- **CardGuard** (this chat assistant)
- The LTB Mobile app: Cards → Report Lost/Stolen
- The 24-hour card hotline: **+94 11 000 0000** (demo number)

## What happens after I report my card?
1. The card is **permanently blocked** right away.
2. You receive a **reference number** (format `LTB-LS-XXXXXX`).
3. A replacement card is ordered automatically, unless you opt out.
4. You get SMS confirmation to your registered mobile number.

## What information do I need to report a lost card?
- Your full name and registered mobile number
- The **last 4 digits** of the card (never the full number)
- The approximate date, time and location you last used the card
- Whether the card was lost or stolen
- Whether you noticed any transactions you don't recognise

## Should I file a police report?
For **stolen** cards, we recommend filing a police report, especially if other items such as your ID were stolen too. A police report number is not required to block the card, but it may be requested during a fraud investigation.

## I found my card after reporting it lost. Can I use it?
No. Once a card has been reported lost or stolen it is permanently blocked and cannot be reactivated. Please cut it up and wait for your replacement. If you only **froze** the card, you can unfreeze it in LTB Mobile.

## Is there a fee for reporting a lost card?
Reporting is free. Replacement card fees are listed in the Card Replacement article.
