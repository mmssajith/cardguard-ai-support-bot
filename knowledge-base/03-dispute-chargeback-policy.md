# Transaction Dispute & Chargeback Policy: Lanka Trust Bank (fictional)

> Demo knowledge-base article for CardGuard. All values are sample data.

## When can I dispute a transaction?
You can dispute a card transaction if:
- **You did not make it** (unauthorized or fraudulent)
- You were **charged twice** for the same purchase
- You were charged the **wrong amount**
- You paid but **goods or services were not delivered**
- You cancelled a subscription but were **still charged**

## How long do I have to raise a dispute?
Within **60 days** of the transaction date shown on your statement.

## Should I contact the merchant first?
For duplicate charges, wrong amounts or non-delivery, we recommend contacting the merchant first, since this is often the fastest fix. For **unauthorized or fraudulent** transactions, do **not** contact the merchant. Report it to us immediately.

## What information do I need?
- Merchant name
- Transaction date
- Transaction amount and currency
- Dispute reason
- Any supporting evidence (receipts, emails, cancellation confirmations)

## What happens after I raise a dispute?
1. You receive a dispute reference number (format `LTB-DP-XXXXXX`).
2. For eligible disputes, a **temporary credit** may be applied within **5 business days**.
3. Our Disputes team investigates with the card network and merchant.
4. Most disputes are resolved within **30–45 days**, and we update you by SMS and email.

## Will my card be blocked?
- **Unauthorized/fraud disputes:** Yes. Your card will be permanently blocked and replaced to stop further fraud.
- **Merchant disputes** (duplicate, wrong amount, non-delivery): No, your card stays active.

## High-value or complex disputes
Disputes above **LKR 100,000** (or the foreign-currency equivalent), or involving multiple transactions, are handled directly by a Fraud & Disputes specialist.
